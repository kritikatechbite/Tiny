Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Scope and purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how TinyMarket Place may process information associated with visits to this website. TinyMarket Place is primarily an informational and editorial publication. The site is designed to explain product categories, practical use cases and comparison considerations rather than operate as a conventional online checkout.

This policy applies to information processed through TinyMarket Place itself. External retailers, manufacturers, hosting services and other third parties may operate under separate privacy notices.

2. Technical information

Normal web infrastructure may process an IP address, browser type, operating system, language settings, requested URLs, referring pages, timestamps, response status and diagnostic information. This data can be needed to deliver pages, detect errors, manage capacity and protect the site.

Technical identifiers do not always identify a person by name, but some may be treated as personal data under applicable law.

3. Information you provide

If you contact the site, you may provide your name, email address, organisation and message content. We ask visitors not to send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact channels.

Information you provide may be used to respond, investigate a correction, resolve a technical issue, maintain relevant correspondence or protect legal rights.

4. Cookies and storage

The current static website does not require behavioural advertising cookies to display its core editorial pages. Basic storage may be introduced for functional preferences such as consent, language or display choices.

If optional analytics, advertising or personalisation technologies are introduced, this policy should be updated so visitors can understand what is used and why.

5. Analytics

TinyMarket Place may use aggregate analytics or hosting-level statistics to understand page popularity, error rates, browser categories, traffic volume and general geographic distribution.

The purpose of analytics is to understand performance and editorial usefulness rather than create unnecessary personal profiles.

6. Security

Technical signals may be processed to detect unusually high request rates, obvious automated abuse, malicious payloads, attempts to access protected paths or denial-of-service patterns.

Security measures are intended to protect legitimate visitors and infrastructure. No automated system is perfect, so controls should be configured proportionately.

7. External links

External links may lead to websites operated by third parties. Those sites may receive standard request information and may use cookies or other technologies according to their own policies.

TinyMarket Place does not control third-party privacy practices merely because a link appears on the site.

8. Legal bases

Where law requires a legal basis, website delivery and security may rely on necessity or legitimate interests, while some optional technologies may require consent.

If consent is the relevant basis, visitors should be given an appropriate way to grant and withdraw consent for future processing.

9. Retention

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed. Technical logs may be retained for operational or security periods determined by providers and configuration.

Correspondence may be retained longer where needed to manage an ongoing issue, document a correction, protect legal rights or meet record-keeping obligations.

10. Security safeguards

Reasonable safeguards can include HTTPS, access controls, reputable hosting and limited administrative access. No system can eliminate every risk.

Visitors should avoid sending confidential credentials through public contact channels and should use care when following third-party links.

11. International processing

Hosting and content-delivery infrastructure may process information in more than one country. As a result, request data can be handled outside the visitor's physical location.

Where law requires safeguards for international transfers, relevant providers or controllers may use contractual or legal mechanisms designed for that purpose.

12. Children

TinyMarket Place is a general-audience informational site and is not designed to solicit personal information from young children.

A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted information inappropriately may contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

13. Privacy rights

Depending on jurisdiction, visitors may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection. These rights can be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

A request should describe the issue clearly. Appropriate verification may be needed before information is disclosed or changed.

14. Browser signals

Browsers and privacy tools may send signals expressing tracking preferences. Standards and legal requirements concerning such signals continue to evolve.

If technologies are introduced for which recognised opt-out signals must be honoured, the site should be configured consistently with applicable requirements.

15. Changes

This policy may be revised when website functionality, providers, legal requirements or privacy practices change.

The current published version should be consulted rather than relying on an old saved copy.

16. Contact

Questions about privacy practices may be directed to the contact information published on the site. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to understand the request.

This policy is intended to provide a transparent overview and is not a substitute for legal advice.
