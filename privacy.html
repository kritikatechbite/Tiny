Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TinyMarket Place may collect, use and protect information associated with visits to this website.

TinyMarket Place is primarily an independent informational website focused on online gaming, digital entertainment, account safety, privacy awareness, device compatibility and healthy gaming habits.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the TinyMarket Place website.

It explains what information may be collected when visitors browse the website, contact us, interact with website features or follow links provided within our informational content.

Third-party gaming platforms, publishers, developers, hosting providers and external websites may operate under their own privacy policies.

2. Nature of our website

TinyMarket Place publishes general information about online gaming and related digital topics.

We do not operate gaming accounts, multiplayer servers, betting platforms, casino services or gambling systems.

Our website is designed primarily to provide educational information rather than process gaming transactions or manage player accounts.

3. Information collected automatically

Standard web infrastructure may automatically process technical information when a visitor accesses the website.

This information may include an IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, language preference, referring page, requested URL, access time, response status and general diagnostic information.

Such information may be required for normal website delivery, performance monitoring, security and troubleshooting.

4. Information you voluntarily provide

Visitors may provide information when contacting TinyMarket Place.

This may include a name, email address, subject line, feedback, correction request or other information included in a message.

We ask visitors not to send passwords, payment card numbers, authentication codes, identity documents or other sensitive account credentials through ordinary contact channels.

5. How we may use information

Information may be used for legitimate website operations, including:

responding to questions, reviewing corrections, improving website content, investigating technical issues, maintaining website security, understanding general website usage and preventing abuse.

We do not need access to a visitor's gaming account to provide general editorial information.

6. Cookies and local storage

TinyMarket Place may use cookies or similar browser storage technologies for basic website functionality.

These technologies may be used for preferences such as language, interface settings or consent choices.

If additional analytics, advertising or personalisation technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy should be updated to reflect those technologies and their purposes.

7. Analytics

TinyMarket Place may use aggregate analytics or hosting-level statistics to understand how the website performs.

Analytics information may include page visits, browser categories, device types, traffic volume, approximate geographic region and technical performance.

The purpose of analytics is generally to improve website performance and understand which informational topics are useful to readers.

8. Website security

Technical information may be processed to protect the website from malicious traffic, automated abuse, suspicious requests, excessive traffic or attempts to access restricted resources.

Security measures may include HTTPS, hosting-provider protections, request monitoring, access controls and other reasonable safeguards.

No security system can guarantee complete protection against every possible threat.

9. Gaming account information

TinyMarket Place does not require visitors to provide gaming account passwords or authentication credentials.

Visitors should never send passwords, one-time security codes, recovery codes or payment credentials when contacting us.

Account-specific issues should be handled through the official support channels of the relevant game or platform.

10. Digital purchases

Our articles may discuss subscriptions, downloadable content, virtual currencies or other digital purchases for informational purposes.

TinyMarket Place does not process third-party game purchases through its informational pages unless a separate service is clearly identified.

Payment details submitted to external gaming stores are governed by the privacy policies and security practices of those third-party services.

11. External links

TinyMarket Place may contain links to external gaming websites, publishers, developers, stores, technical resources or other third-party services.

When visitors leave TinyMarket Place, the external website may receive standard technical information and may use cookies or other technologies.

TinyMarket Place does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

12. Third-party services

The website may rely on third-party service providers for functions such as hosting, content delivery, analytics, email delivery or security.

Such providers may process limited information when necessary to provide their services.

Their processing may also be governed by their own terms and privacy documentation.

13. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, information may be processed for purposes such as providing the website, responding to requested communications, protecting security or pursuing legitimate operational interests.

Certain optional technologies may require consent depending on the visitor's jurisdiction and the nature of the technology.

14. Consent

Where consent is legally required for a particular technology or type of processing, visitors should be given an appropriate method to provide that consent.

Where required, visitors should also be able to withdraw consent for future processing.

15. Data retention

Information should generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Technical logs may be retained for periods determined by hosting or security providers.

Contact correspondence may be retained where reasonably necessary to manage an enquiry, correction request or legal obligation.

16. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures may be used to protect website information.

These measures may include encrypted connections, restricted administrative access, reputable infrastructure providers and security monitoring.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

17. International data processing

Web hosting, analytics and content-delivery infrastructure may operate across multiple countries.

As a result, technical information associated with a website visit may be processed outside the visitor's current country.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, relevant service providers may use recognised contractual or legal mechanisms.

18. Children's privacy

TinyMarket Place is a general informational website and does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise younger users' online activity and review the privacy and communication settings of gaming services used by children.

A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information to TinyMarket Place may contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

19. Parental controls

Some gaming devices and platforms provide parental controls for purchases, communication, downloads and screen time.

TinyMarket Place may discuss these features for informational purposes, but the actual controls are managed by the relevant third-party platform.

20. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and jurisdiction, visitors may have privacy rights relating to personal information.

These rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to certain forms of processing.

Legal conditions and exceptions may apply to individual requests.

21. Privacy requests

A privacy request should clearly explain the information or issue concerned.

Appropriate verification may be required before personal information is disclosed, corrected or deleted.

Visitors should not send unnecessary sensitive information while making a privacy request.

22. Browser privacy signals

Browsers and privacy tools may provide settings or signals intended to communicate user tracking preferences.

Standards and legal obligations regarding these signals may change over time.

Where applicable law requires recognised signals to be honoured, website technologies should be configured accordingly.

23. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a Do Not Track setting.

Because industry standards and legal interpretations may differ, the effect of these settings can vary across websites and technologies.

24. Advertising technologies

The core informational pages of TinyMarket Place do not require behavioural advertising technologies to display their content.

If advertising or marketing technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and any relevant consent mechanism should be updated accordingly.

25. Email communications

If a visitor contacts TinyMarket Place by email, their email address and message content may be processed in order to respond.

We may retain relevant correspondence for reasonable administrative, editorial or security purposes.

26. Spam and abuse prevention

Technical measures may be used to identify spam, automated submissions, malicious requests or other abusive activity.

Information processed for security purposes should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for protecting the website and its users.

27. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when website functionality, service providers, technical practices or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with the applicable update date.

28. Independent status

TinyMarket Place is an independent informational website.

References to game developers, publishers, platforms or brands do not imply that those organisations control TinyMarket Place's privacy practices.

29. No betting or gambling account data

TinyMarket Place does not operate betting, wagering, casino or gambling accounts.

Our general online gaming content does not require visitors to submit gambling account credentials, wagering information or betting history.

30. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices associated with TinyMarket Place may be sent through the Contact page.

Please avoid including passwords, payment details, authentication codes or other sensitive information that is not necessary for us to understand your request.
