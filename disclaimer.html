1. Independent editorial website

TinyMarket Place is an independent editorial site and is not presented as the official website of any manufacturer, retailer or marketplace unless expressly stated.

Third-party references do not automatically imply sponsorship, endorsement or partnership.

2. General information

Content is provided for general informational purposes. It may discuss product features, use cases, room organisation, travel considerations and everyday accessories.

General information cannot account for every visitor's circumstances.

3. No personalised recommendation

A statement that a product category may be useful in a situation is not personalised purchasing advice.

Readers should consider their own requirements and verify material specifications before making decisions.

4. Product changes

Manufacturers and retailers may change specifications, packaging, materials, dimensions, warranties, prices and availability.

Current details should be verified with authoritative sources.

5. Images

Images may be editorial or illustrative and may communicate a category or environment without depicting a specific recommended product.

Do not infer exact specifications, availability or commercial relationships from an image alone.

6. External links

External links can lead to websites controlled by third parties. TinyMarket Place cannot guarantee their accuracy, security, pricing or customer-service practices.

Visitors should review the linked site's terms and policies independently.

7. Pricing and availability

Prices, stock, taxes, shipping, regional restrictions and promotional conditions can change.

Rely on current information from the actual seller at the time of purchase.

8. Compatibility

Technology and accessory compatibility should not be assumed from a general article. Model numbers, connectors, operating systems and regional variants may matter.

Consult official documentation when compatibility is important.

9. Installation

Home products may involve measurements, weight limits, electrical requirements or installation instructions. General guidance cannot determine whether a particular installation is safe in a particular property.

Follow manufacturer instructions and consult a qualified professional where safety or structural matters are involved.

10. Travel

Travel rules vary by carrier, airport, country and security authority. Size limits, battery rules and prohibited items can change.

Confirm current rules with the relevant provider or authority before travelling.

11. Health and safety

TinyMarket Place does not provide medical or occupational-safety advice.

Read product instructions, warnings, age guidance and care requirements before use.

12. Environmental statements

Terms such as sustainable, recyclable or eco-friendly can depend on definitions, local systems and evidence supplied by manufacturers.

Where environmental performance matters, review specific evidence and local disposal options.

13. Opinions

Editorial assessments can involve judgement. Different users may reasonably value different factors.

Opinion should be understood as opinion rather than a universal factual conclusion.

14. No guarantee of results

Using a particular organisational product, desk accessory, travel item or routine does not guarantee a specific outcome.

Results depend on user behaviour, environment and many other factors.

15. Third-party rights

Third-party trademarks, names and copyrighted material belong to their respective owners.

Their appearance in informational content does not transfer ownership or create an implied affiliation.

16. Availability and errors

The website may experience temporary errors, broken links, hosting interruptions or outdated content.

Material may be corrected, changed or removed without prior notice.

17. Reliance

Readers should use TinyMarket Place as one source of general information rather than the sole authority for an important decision.

Important claims should be verified with primary sources and professional advice used when appropriate.

18. Corrections

Specific correction requests are welcome. Identify the page, statement and a reliable source where possible.

This disclaimer explains the limits of a general editorial website and does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
