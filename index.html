How TinyMarket Place works

We start with the situation rather than the product list. That means asking where an item will be used, how much space is available, whether it needs to travel, how often it will be handled and which details should be verified before buying. Our pages are informational and readers should confirm specifications, availability and seller policies directly with the relevant retailer or manufacturer.

What makes a useful comparison

Useful comparisons explain trade-offs. A smaller product may save space but hold less. A lighter travel item may be easier to carry but less durable. A desk accessory may improve organisation but be unnecessary if a setup already solves the same problem. We focus on those practical questions instead of claiming that one choice is universally best.
