CONTACT TINYMARKET PLACE

Questions, corrections or feedback?

TinyMarket Place is an independent informational website focused on online gaming, account safety, device compatibility, privacy, digital purchases and healthier gaming habits.

If you have a question about our content, notice information that may need updating or want to suggest a topic for a future gaming guide, you can contact us using the information below.

General enquiries

For general questions about TinyMarket Place, our editorial approach or the topics covered on the website, please contact:

Email: hello@tinymarket.example

Please include a clear subject line so we can understand the nature of your enquiry.

Content corrections

Online games and gaming platforms can change frequently. Features, device requirements, subscriptions, privacy settings and platform policies may be updated over time.

If you believe information on TinyMarket Place is outdated or inaccurate, please send us the following details:

Page title or URL, the specific section you believe should be reviewed, a short explanation of the issue and, where possible, a reference to an official publisher or platform source.

We may review submitted information and update content where appropriate.

Suggest a gaming topic

We welcome suggestions for informational gaming topics that may be useful to readers.

Suggested topics may include account security, multiplayer basics, device compatibility, game genres, parental controls, privacy settings, digital purchases, game updates or healthy gaming habits.

Topic suggestions do not guarantee publication, but they can help us understand which areas readers would like explained in greater detail.

Game and account support

TinyMarket Place does not operate individual games, gaming platforms or player accounts.

We cannot reset passwords, recover gaming accounts, process refunds, investigate bans, change subscriptions or resolve technical problems on third-party platforms.

For account-specific or technical support, please contact the official game publisher, developer or platform provider directly.

Privacy when contacting us

Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, authentication codes or other sensitive account information through email.

We do not need your gaming account password or payment credentials to review general editorial feedback or correction requests.

For additional information about how website data may be handled, please review our Privacy Policy.

Response times

We aim to review genuine editorial enquiries and correction requests within a reasonable period. Response times may vary depending on the nature of the request.

Messages that are unrelated to our informational content, contain spam or request support for third-party gaming accounts may not receive a response.

Independent informational website

TinyMarket Place is an independent online gaming information resource. Unless clearly stated otherwise, we are not affiliated with or endorsed by individual game developers, publishers, console manufacturers or gaming platforms.

Our website focuses on general gaming information and digital entertainment awareness. We do not provide betting, wagering or gambling services.
